## Orbit la gi?

Orbit la mot framework dieu phoi da tac tu (multi-agent orchestration) goc Python. No cung cap bo cong cu day du de xay dung, dieu phoi va quan ly cac luong cong viec tac tu AI voi 30+ vai tro tac tu chuyen biet, 35+ ky nang tai su dung va mot engine runtime de phoi hop nhom.

## Tinh nang

- **30 vai tro tac tu** — Tac tu chuyen biet cho moi nhiem vu: `$architect`, `$executor`, `$debugger`, `$code-reviewer`, `$planner` va nhieu hon
- **35+ ky nang** — Ky nang luong cong viec tai su dung: `$plan`, `$team`, `$autopilot`, `$ralph`, `$deep-interview`, `$doctor`
- **Engine runtime** — Runtime dua tren event-sourcing voi quan ly quyen han, dieu phoi va phoi hop hop thu
- **Dieu phoi nhom** — Phoi hop da tac tu dua tren tmux voi dinh tuyen theo vai tro
- **Sparkshell** — Thuc thi lenh voi tom tat dau ra bang AI
- **Explorer** — Kham pha co so ma nguon an toan voi cac lenh duoc phep
- **HUD** — Bang trang thai tac tu thoi gian thuc
- **Pipeline** — Pipeline thuc thi da giai doan co the cau hinh
- **Autoresearch** — Nhiem vu nghien cuu tu dong voi hop dong danh gia

## Bat dau nhanh

```bash
pip install orbit
orbit summary
orbit doctor
orbit agents
orbit skills
orbit ask "review security" --role architect
orbit setup
```
